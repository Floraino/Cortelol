# Product Requirements Document
# 01 Product Overview
- **Product Name:** Cortelol
- **Tagline:** Turn long podcast episodes into reviewed, captioned short videos ready to publish.
- **Description:** Cortelol is an open source desktop application for independent podcasters, editors, and creators. It imports a local recording, uses Gemini to transcribe speech and a user-selected Claude or OpenAI model to propose strong moments, then helps the user edit, caption, export, and publish short clips. The creator keeps the source media and project files locally and supplies their own API credentials.

# 02 Problem
Finding compelling moments in a long recording, cutting them accurately, reframing video, correcting captions, and preparing platform-specific copy takes hours. Existing workflows scatter the transcript, editor, export settings, and publishing across several tools. AI suggestions can also invent quotes or select moments that lack context, so the creator needs a fast way to verify every proposed clip against the original recording before release.

# 03 Goal
Enable a creator to import a 60-minute podcast, review AI suggestions, and export at least three captioned vertical clips within 30 minutes of completed transcription, excluding provider processing and upload time. Treat this as a product target to validate with real users, not a guaranteed processing time.

# 04 Target Users
- Independent podcasters repurposing their own episodes for social platforms.
- Solo creators and small teams producing interview, discussion, or educational video.
- Video editors who want transcript-assisted discovery while retaining control over every cut.

# 05 Core Features (Version 1)
- **Local project and media import:** Create a project, import a local video or audio file, inspect duration and format, and keep source media on the user's machine. Audio-only sources can use a simple visual background for vertical export.
- **Gemini transcription:** Transcribe supported audio with timestamps and speaker labels where available. Show progress, estimated provider cost when available, failures, and a correction interface. Split recordings into supported chunks, preserve source offsets, and retain an editable transcript.
- **Long-form transcription option (scope extension):** Offer ElevenLabs Scribe v2 as a user-selected transcription provider for long recordings. Show a continuous, readable transcript while retaining timed phrases for clip selection and captions. Keep provider selection and consent explicit: ElevenLabs handles uploaded audio under its own retention terms, and Cortelol must explain that before sending. Preserve existing Gemini jobs and local corrections when adding the option.
- **AI moment discovery:** Send transcript excerpts and timing metadata to a user-selected Claude or OpenAI provider. Return a ranked list with start and end time, hook, rationale, and source quote. Validate proposed ranges against the transcript and let users accept, reject, or adjust them. Scores are editorial hints, never promises of virality.
- **Developed clip duration (scope clarification):** Discovery should propose coherent 2–5 minute excerpts with a setup, developed idea, and usable ending, rather than isolated 15-second hooks. The model's quote is a source anchor inside the proposed cut, not the full clip transcript. The creator can still trim or create a shorter cut manually during review. Local preview and export must support the full proposed duration.
- **DeepSeek discovery option (scope extension):** Offer DeepSeek as another user-selected text provider for moment discovery, using the same bounded corrected-transcript excerpts, validation, review controls, privacy consent, and locally stored API credentials as Claude and OpenAI. DeepSeek does not replace Gemini transcription or add automatic publishing.
- **Editorial discovery quality (scope clarification):** Rank 2–5 minute candidates by a specific, transcript-supported reason to watch or listen: a surprising claim, useful insight, clear disagreement, emotional turn, concrete story, or well-developed answer. Show the viewer promise, the evidence in the source, and why the excerpt stands alone. Do not equate controversy with quality or invent conflict, claims, or audience outcomes. The creator reviews every recommendation and can reject or change it.
- **Suggested vertical framing (scope extension):** For video clips, let the creator select local face analysis or Gemini visual analysis per project. Analyze only a small bounded sample of frames and suggest an editable 9:16 horizontal center when a subject can be identified. Local analysis is the default. Gemini analysis requires explicit consent for each request and sends only sampled frames, without audio or the full recording. Show uncertainty when faces move or multiple speakers compete for the crop. Never alter the recording or apply a framing decision to export without review.
- **Framing over time (scope extension):** Let the creator review and edit framing choices at points within a clip. A crop may follow different subjects over time; a wide shot may switch to a fit layout so participants remain visible in a 9:16 export. Suggestions remain bounded samples and require explicit application and review. Preserve existing single-position clip drafts.
- **AI-derived captions (scope clarification):** Use the selected speech AI provider's timed transcript and the creator's corrections to generate synchronized, editable subtitle phrases for each clip. Keep source timing and spoken meaning; do not invent dialogue. Any optional editorial overlay or summary text requires a separate review from faithful subtitles.
- **Clip editor:** Preview the source and selected cut, trim boundaries, choose a vertical crop or background, adjust framing, and maintain readable safe areas for overlays and platform controls. Support a straightforward 9:16 workflow first.
- **Captions:** Generate synchronized captions from the corrected transcript, offer editable text and timing, provide a small set of visual presets, and burn captions into exported video. Export subtitle files separately when useful.
- **Title and hashtag drafts:** Generate editable titles, descriptions, and platform-specific hashtag suggestions from each approved clip. Do not present invented claims, names, or quotes as facts.
- **Export and publishing:** Export MP4 clips locally. After an explicit review and publish action, support official TikTok and Instagram publishing integrations for eligible accounts and approved apps. Show account eligibility, required permissions, platform processing state, and clear errors; always offer manual export when direct publishing is unavailable. Publishing is never triggered automatically by a model response.
- **Job recovery and privacy controls:** Resume interrupted local projects, retry failed provider or render jobs, show external data sent to each provider, and allow deletion of local projects and temporary files.

# 06 Out of Scope (for now)
- Guaranteed viral performance, engagement prediction, or automated posting without user review.
- Multi-user collaboration, cloud project sync, hosted media storage, or a paid subscription service.
- A full nonlinear video editor with arbitrary tracks, effects, and complex motion graphics.
- Scraping or browser automation to bypass social platform publishing APIs, permissions, or review.
- Licensed music discovery or automatic clearance of third-party podcast footage and audio.

# 07 Success Metrics
- At least 80% of successful imports reach a usable, timestamped transcript without a manual restart in pilot sessions.
- At least 70% of pilot users export one reviewed, captioned clip from their first imported episode.
- Median hands-on time from completed transcription to three exported clips is 30 minutes or less for a 60-minute episode in pilot sessions.
- At least 90% of exported clips pass a manual spot check for caption synchronization and intended start and end boundaries.
- Publishing failures always surface a specific status and leave the local exported file available.

# 08 Initial Open Source Release Scope Decision
The initial Windows open source release uses reviewed local MP4 and SRT export as the publishing handoff. The creator uploads those files manually through the destination platform. Direct TikTok and Instagram posting remains a planned version 1 integration after a developer app has the required approval, OAuth connection, and platform capabilities; no approved app is available for the current release. The interface must not offer a live publish action without those prerequisites. The local export workflow and its review, caption, and source-preservation requirements remain in scope for release acceptance.

# 09 Public Landing Page Scope Decision
The project includes a static, multilingual public landing page hosted on GitHub Pages. It explains the local podcast-to-clip workflow with illustrative, synthetic interface scenes and links to the public source code. The page may link to GitHub Releases, but it must not offer an installer download or claim a production release until the Windows release checklist is complete and a reviewed installer is published. The site does not collect recordings, credentials, analytics, or contact information.

# 10 Authorized Desktop Scope Extensions

The acquisition item below is the earlier scope and implementation snapshot. Section 11 records the proposed replacement and the technical boundary for a YouTube-only revision; localization and local transcription remain unchanged by that proposal.

- **Desktop language selector and multilingual copy:** Support `en`, `pt-BR`, `es`, `fr`, `de`, `it`, `ar`, `hi`, `zh-CN`, and `ja` in the desktop language selector. Apply the selection to navigation, settings, import, jobs, transcript review, discovery, editing, export, errors, consent, and accessibility labels, including right-to-left layout where appropriate for Arabic. Persist the preference locally and visibly fall back to English for missing translations until catalogs are complete. Language selection changes interface copy only; it must not silently translate source transcripts, captions, generated copy, or media.
- **User-authorized retrieval of owned videos:** Let a creator bring in a video they uploaded or otherwise have the right to download. YouTube Studio and Google Takeout are the official paths for downloading the creator's own YouTube uploads; the resulting local file can be imported. Vimeo permits downloads from its own download control and offers direct file links to owners; the desktop app may retrieve a user-supplied official Vimeo direct file link after explicit rights and method confirmation. Constrain each HTTPS request, redirect, DNS resolution, response size, and destination before writing the local file, and persist a provenance receipt without storing signed URL tokens. Do not expose a general video-page extractor or yt-dlp command in the desktop app. User ownership alone does not establish platform permission. Offer local-file import when retrieval is unavailable. Do not bypass DRM, authentication, age restrictions, geographic restrictions, paywalls, rate limits, or other access controls; do not collect site credentials or cookies. Recheck the current rules of each site before enabling its URL path. See [YouTube creator downloads](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/56100), [Google Takeout](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190), and [Vimeo direct file links](https://help.vimeo.com/hc/en-us/articles/12426150952593-How-to-access-my-video-s-direct-links).
- **Local transcription in the desktop app:** Add an optional local provider using the existing speech-to-text script for creators who install Python and its dependency and supply an existing local model directory. The desktop app must validate the executable, model path, chunk output and timestamps; show setup, progress, cancellation and errors; save completed chunks in the existing project database; and send the resulting phrases through the same mandatory correction and caption review as hosted providers. Do not describe Python or the model as bundled unless release packaging actually includes them. The script reads the source without modifying it and writes timed JSON, SRT, and quality flags locally. No ASR system, local or hosted, can guarantee 100 percent accuracy. Document setup, resource needs, language options, and the limits of automated quality flags.

Acceptance requires a persistent language choice across restarts, complete critical consent and error copy in all ten locales, and no URL retrieval without explicit user authorization and a permitted method. The local ASR desktop flow requires documented optional setup, validated local outputs, resumable saved chunks, and mandatory human review. Existing projects and corrections must remain readable.

Implementation status: the ten-language static catalog and selector are implemented. Dynamic backend job messages may still display in English. The acquisition panel exposes official YouTube Studio/Takeout and Vimeo handoffs plus bounded official Vimeo direct-link retrieval; no general YouTube URL extractor is mounted. The local ASR bridge saves chunks and routes phrases through correction review. Python, faster-whisper, and model weights are not bundled. Real-model, live Vimeo, and clean desktop acceptance remain open.

# 11 Proposed YouTube-only Acquisition Scope Change

Proposal recorded on 2026-10-27, before implementation: replace the Vimeo acquisition scope with a YouTube-only downloader accepting public videos from any channel. This describes the requested product direction, not an approved technical method or a promise that every public video can be downloaded. Public visibility alone does not establish copyright permission or permission to download through a particular method. Current official YouTube download guidance and terms must be reviewed before defining the technical boundary. Until that boundary is documented, keep general YouTube URL extraction disabled. Existing local imports, projects, source files, exports, and acquisition receipts must remain usable. Section 10 records the earlier acquisition scope and implementation snapshot; this proposal does not claim the existing Vimeo code has been removed.

Official-source review completed on 2026-10-27:

- [YouTube Terms of Service, Permissions and Restrictions](https://www.youtube.com/static?template=terms): downloading requires express service authorization or prior written permission from YouTube and applicable rights holders. The terms also restrict automated access and circumvention. An ownership declaration or a public URL does not establish those permissions.
- [Download videos you uploaded](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/56100?hl=en) documents Studio and Takeout for the creator's own uploads and says other users' videos cannot be downloaded through that workflow. Even own-upload downloads have eligibility and frequency limits. [Google Takeout guidance](https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/3024190?hl=en) describes exporting account data.
- [Premium offline viewing](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/11977233?hl=en) describes playback within YouTube, with account and availability checks. The [offline FAQ](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7381437?hl=en) explains encrypted storage and playback within YouTube. These features are not documented as editable MP4 export for Cortelol.
- [YouTube API Developer Policies, III.E.1](https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies#e.-handling-youtube-data-and-content) restrict API clients from downloading or storing audiovisual content without YouTube's prior written approval. API credentials or OAuth do not establish approval for this proposed downloader.

Technical boundary defined after that review: the supported YouTube acquisition route for the proposed revision is a user-operated Studio/Takeout download followed by local-file import. A creator from another channel can supply an authorized local recording directly. Cortelol must not treat public visibility, a rights checkbox, Creative Commons labeling, or a Premium subscription as sufficient evidence that an arbitrary YouTube extraction method is permitted. No general public-video downloader is authorized by this proposal alone. This is a product and engineering boundary based on the reviewed documentation, not a legal determination or a guarantee of compliance, permission, availability, download success, or rights clearance.

If the YouTube-only revision proceeds, remove Vimeo controls and reject Vimeo and other non-YouTube acquisition requests at the Rust command boundary; retain existing local media and provenance receipts. Do not repurpose the Vimeo direct-link downloader by accepting YouTube pages or CDN links. Keep an arbitrary-channel URL downloader pending until a specific documented or expressly authorized method covers that use and has bounded network and filesystem behavior. Record that method, permissions, data handling, supported cases, errors, and verification here before enabling it. Do not add cookies, login collection, browser scraping, undocumented player endpoints, third-party extraction services, or access-control workarounds as a fallback.

Acceptance for the bounded revision: show YouTube-only acquisition guidance and a usable local-file import fallback; expose no Vimeo acquisition action or callable transfer; send no media-page, metadata, or CDN request for unsupported URLs; preserve prior projects and media; make unsupported cases explicit in every supported locale. The requested ability to download any public video from any channel remains unfulfilled and proposed. Documentation changes alone do not satisfy this acceptance or claim the existing application already follows the revised boundary.

## 11.1 Public-video downloader direction after user correction

On 2026-10-27, the user explicitly chose to pursue downloads of public YouTube videos from any channel without using YouTube policy compliance as an implementation gate. This supersedes the permission-based stop condition above for the *new engineering plan*; the official-source review remains historical context and is not a claim of authorization. The goal is to accept a public video page URL, save a supported ordinary video locally, and import it into Cortelol. Do not promise every public video will work or describe the method as YouTube-approved. Keep login-only, DRM, age, geographic, paywall, and other access-controlled content outside the first supported subset; do not collect credentials or browser cookies.

The current local test candidate accepts one canonical YouTube video URL and runs packaged yt-dlp and Deno executables with bounded time and staging, cancellation, FFprobe validation, and a non-overwriting file and checksum receipt. The Import screen calls the existing `import_media` path after download and keeps the saved file for retry if import fails. It does not guarantee any particular public video or establish legal rights. Windows process and network containment, a broker for each request, adversarial native tests, and live acceptance remain open engineering gates in [PLAN.md](PLAN.md#youtube-public-video-downloader-plan-2026-10-27). This candidate must not be described as a reviewed public release.
